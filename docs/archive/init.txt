Here’s the full sequence, top to bottom.

Opening Sequence (12–15 min)

	1.	Seated Forward Fold — Apostles’ Creed, 3–5 min. Legs extended, fold forward, soften head and shoulders toward the earth.
	2.	Child’s Pose — first Our Father, 3 min. Hips sink toward heels, arms extended, forehead resting.
	3.	Supported Butterfly — three Hail Marys, 5 min. On the back, soles together, knees falling open. Blankets under the knees if needed.
	4.	Gentle Spinal Twist — Glory Be, 2 min each side. Knee drawn across the body, shoulders flat. Alternate sides.
	5.	Savasana threshold — 1–2 min. Palms up, body softened. The still moment before the mysteries begin.

The Five Decades (18–25 min)

Each decade follows the same pattern: Pose A during the Our Father, Pose B during the ten Hail Marys, Pose C during the Glory Be.

	•	Pose A — Child’s Pose. 1–2 min. Same every decade. The body learns the cue.
	•	Pose B — the deep hold. ~5 min. Changes each decade in fixed order (below).
	•	Pose C — neutral on the back. 1 min. Palms up, breath steady, transition into the next decade.

The Five Deep Holds (Pose B), in order:

	1.	Reclined Figure-Four — hips and glutes. Lie on your back, cross right ankle over left thigh, draw left thigh toward chest. Switch sides halfway.
	2.	Supported Bridge — hip flexors and chest. Lie on your back, knees bent, lift hips and slide a block or bolster under the sacrum. Let the weight rest on the prop.
	3.	Happy Baby — low back and inner hips. Lie on your back, knees toward chest, grab the outside edges of your feet, gently pull knees toward armpits.
	4.	Reclined Spinal Twist — spine and digestion. Lie on your back, draw one knee across the body, shoulders stay flat. Switch sides halfway.
	5.	Legs Up the Wall — recovery and sleep. Lie on your back with legs extended up a wall, or hips elevated on a blanket. Hold for the full decade as your closer.

Total: 30–40 minutes.
