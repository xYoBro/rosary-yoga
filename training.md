# Rosary Yoga — The Training

*The daytime counterpart to the nightly practice. Strength, conditioning, and skill, organized by the same rosary the body prays at night.*

---

## What this is

[`practice.md`](practice.md) is the nightly anchor — yin yoga and the rosary, thirty to forty minutes before sleep, calibrated for mobility and rest. It is explicit that it is not strength work.

This document is the strength work. It is the practice of the day's work hour, before the body becomes too tired to lift. It uses the same rosary structure, the same weekly mystery rotation, and the same long-arc patience. The day's mystery is contemplated twice — once in the morning's work, once in the evening's stillness. The body sees both faces of it.

The training is calibrated for a condo apartment with downstairs neighbors: no jumping, no impact, no equipment beyond the floor and a wall. The benchmark is the U.S. Air Force Physical Fitness Test — pushups, situps, plank, 1.5-mile run — chosen because it is publicly scored, age-graded, and built from movements that fit the room.

The goal is to still be doing this in thirty years.

---

## The rosary, walked instead of prayed

`practice.md` treats the rosary as a prayer object — fifty-nine beads, an opening pendant, five decades. This document treats the same shape as a **map**.

Each bead is one week. The body lives on one bead at a time and advances one bead each Sunday. Across a year and change, the body walks the full rosary.

```
                          [Centerpiece — Mary]
                         /                    \
                   [OF₂ — Threshold]      [Decade 1 — Annunciation]
                        |                       |
                   [HM · HM · HM]          [Decade 2 — Visitation]
                        |                       |
                   [OF₁ — Cross]           [Decade 3 — Nativity]
                        |                       |
                   [Crucifix — entry]      [Decade 4 — Presentation]
                                                |
                                           [Decade 5 — Finding in the Temple]
                                                |
                                           (return to centerpiece)
```

- **Crucifix.** Entry. Week zero. The body is examined, the room is set, the notebook is opened.
- **Opening pendant** (six beads). Seven weeks of onboarding. The body is introduced to the work without being broken by it.
- **Five decades** (eleven beads each, fifty-five beads total). The year's main journey. Each decade is dedicated to one mystery and ends in one boss.

Total nodes: sixty-two. Total time at canonical pace: approximately fourteen months. The rosary is the journey shape; the calendar is incidental. Missed weeks are not failures, they are weeks the bead has not yet advanced.

## The week — daily liturgy

The day of the week determines the modality of work. The rotation is the rosary's traditional mystery rotation, mapped onto the body.

| Day | Mystery | Modality | What it is |
|-----|---------|----------|------------|
| Monday | Joyful | Mobility | The Vigil and an extended flow. Joints, breath, light movement. *Receiving the news.* |
| Tuesday | Sorrowful | Endurance | The grind. Run intervals, pushup ladders, the work that suffers. *Agony in the garden.* |
| Wednesday | Glorious | Strength | Heavier resistance. Pullup progressions, single-leg work, lower reps, higher quality. *Lifting up.* |
| Thursday | Luminous | Skill | Technique day. The skill ladder. Slow, focused, learning. *Illumination.* |
| Friday | Sorrowful | Endurance | The other half of conditioning. If Tuesday ran, Friday calisthenics-grinds. *The long carrying.* |
| Saturday | Joyful | Recovery flow | Lightest day. Walk, gentle work, breath. *The presentation.* |
| Sunday | — | **Sanctum** | Rest. The Custodian's day. Logbook entries. Plan the next bead. |

The night does not change. After the day's work, `practice.md` runs as written — same poses, same order, same thirty to forty minutes. The body learns the cue.

A note on the lopsidedness. Two endurance days, two mobility days, one strength day, one skill day. The AF PT test components are all conditioning movements; they live cleanly under Sorrowful and Glorious. Strength once a week is sufficient for a bodyweight program in Year One. The lopsidedness is real, and it is correct for the room.

## The decade — a periodization cycle

Each decade is ten beads of work plus one boss bead. Ten weeks is the canonical length of a progressive-overload block, and the rosary already counts that way. The decade does the periodization arithmetic for you.

| Bead | Phase | Load relative to foundation |
|------|-------|-----------------------------|
| 1 | Foundation | 100% — patterning, learning the movements |
| 2 | Build | +10% |
| 3 | Build | +20% |
| 4 | Deload | Drop to bead-1 levels. The rosary breathes. |
| 5 | Build | +30% from foundation |
| 6 | Build | +40% |
| 7 | Build | +50% |
| 8 | Peak | Highest volume of the decade |
| 9 | Taper | Volume drops, intensity preserved |
| 10 | Sharpening | Light, fast, focused. The body remembers. |
| **OF** | **Boss** | **The fight.** |

Three layers fix the workout, with no overlap:

1. **The bead** fixes the *load*.
2. **The day** fixes the *modality*.
3. **The dice** rolls the specific *exercise* within the modality.

A worked example. Decade 3 (Nativity), Bead 8 (peak), Tuesday (Sorrowful):

- *Warmup.* The Vigil.
- *Dice rolls.* 400-metre repeats.
- *Work.* Eight repetitions at threshold pace, ninety seconds rest.
- *Finisher.* Five sets of twenty-five pushups, five sets of forty-five-second plank.

The same Tuesday on Bead 2 of the same decade would be four repetitions, three sets of ten pushups, three sets of twenty-second plank. Same shape, lighter load. The body is being walked toward the Anvil.

## The pendant — seven weeks of onboarding

The opening pendant is not a watered-down decade. It is a different ritual. The body is being installed in the practice.

| Bead | Name | What happens |
|------|------|--------------|
| 0 | Crucifix | Week zero. Read the manual. Set up the room. Open the notebook. No work. |
| 1 | First Hail Mary | Mobility only. The Vigil, daily, for seven days. |
| 2 | Second Hail Mary | Mobility plus a daily walk. |
| 3 | Third Hail Mary | Add five pushups, five situps, five wall-sits, once daily. The body remembers it has muscles. |
| 4 | **OF₁ — The Cross** | First trial. Ten pushups, ten situps, a quarter-mile walk. Recorded. |
| 5 | Centerpiece — Mary | The Custodian introduces themselves in full. A quiet week. |
| 6 | **OF₂ — The Threshold** | Half of an AF PT test. Pushups to fatigue, situps to fatigue, plank to fatigue, half-mile run. Recorded. Year One begins on the next bead. |

The two pendant trials are not bosses — they are baselines, scored against nothing but themselves. The first boss is The Sentinel, at the end of Decade 1.

## The bosses

Bosses are location-bound. Each lives at the entrance of a decade's eleventh bead. They are reached by walking the ten preceding beads. They are also time-pressured: if the practitioner lingers more than six weeks past the boss bead — through illness, travel, avoidance — the Custodian announces an *intercession*, and the next compatible workout becomes the boss whether the practitioner feels ready or not.

The staged escalation:

| Weeks since reaching the boss bead | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–4 | Silence. The boss is dormant. The practitioner chooses the moment. |
| 5 | A *rumor* — a paragraph in the logbook. *"Something stirs at the Forge. I can hear it from here."* |
| 6+ | *Intercession.* The next compatible workout becomes the boss. The fight begins. |

A defeated boss can be re-fought any Sunday Sanctum for a personal-record attempt, or summoned as a *haunt* by the dice on a low probability (~2% per day) — an optional re-fight the practitioner can decline.

### Year One — The Joyful Mysteries

| Decade | Mystery | Boss | The fight |
|--------|---------|------|-----------|
| 1 | The Annunciation | **The Sentinel** | Full AF PT test. Baseline scouting. No win condition — the score is the score. |
| 2 | The Visitation | **The Mirror** | Full PT test. Beat any one category from The Sentinel. |
| 3 | The Nativity | **The Anvil** | No run. Pushups, pullup progressions, situps, plank — each to fatigue. Total reps plus plank time equals score. |
| 4 | The Presentation | **The Pacer** | 1.5-mile run, target time. Cardio in isolation. AF "Excellent" pace for the practitioner's age and gender bracket. |
| 5 | The Finding in the Temple | **The Standard** | Full PT test, scored to AF "Excellent" thresholds across all four components. |

Each boss is named once in the Custodian's voice when first announced, and that name carries forward in the codex.

### Years Two through Four

The four mystery sets are pre-architected by tradition. The structural decisions for these years can wait until Year One is nearly walked.

| Year | Mystery set | Theme | Boss flavor (sketch) |
|------|-------------|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | Joyful | Beginning. Receiving the body. | Baselines, mirrors, basic standards. |
| 2 | Sorrowful | Suffering through volume. | Hybrid long-form fights — Murph-style. The Crucible. Longer, harder. |
| 3 | Glorious | Mastery and elevation. | "Excellent+" thresholds. Personal records across the board. The body flies. |
| 4 | Luminous | Transformation and teaching. | New disciplines added. The practice turns outward — perhaps coaching another. |

That is a four-year arc with built-in narrative escalation. The Church did the design work.

## The Vigil

The morning warmup. Approximately ten minutes. Done at home on a carpeted condo floor, no equipment, no impact.

What the Vigil must do:

- Prepare the body for the day's modality, whatever it is
- Be memorizable in a week, run without a sheet
- Leave the body warm and present, not drained
- Reset the spine, the hips, and the shoulders

## The dice

A small randomizer — a physical die kept by the notebook, or a tap on the app. The dice roll within the day's modality. They never override it.

On a Sorrowful day the dice picks between the day's compatible exercises — intervals, ladders, AMRAPs — but never substitutes a strength day. The bead's load and the boss schedule are likewise outside the dice's reach.

What the dice owns:

- The specific exercise within the day's modality
- Whether the day adds a small modifier (eccentric tempo, single-arm, paused reps)
- Whether a *haunt* occurs (rare, ~2%) — an optional re-fight of a beaten boss

Why the dice. Sameness is the enemy of long practices. Without variation the body stops learning. With variation the body cannot get bored.

## The Custodian

The voice of the training. Speaks in liturgical cadence. Reads the mystery at the start of each day in the logbook header. Announces bosses with a fixed formula. Records, does not motivate. Witnesses, does not coach.

A boss announcement, on the eve of the fight:

> *On the eve of the tenth bead of the third decade, the Forge opens. The Anvil waits at the threshold. Your Nativity ends tomorrow at dawn.*

A morning header, on a Tuesday in the second decade:

> *Tuesday of the second decade. The Visitation continues. The day is Sorrowful. Mary walks to Elizabeth. You walk into the work.*

A logbook entry, after a boss fight:

> *The Sentinel was met on the seventh week of the third month. Pushups: 32. Situps: 41. Plank: 1:18. Run: 14:42. The score is the score. Decade 2 begins on the next bead.*

The Custodian writes by hand. The codex is paper. The app is for the prayers; the journal is for the work.

## The codex and the logbook

Two paper documents. The app does not need to know about them.

**The Codex.** A single booklet, one spread per boss. Name, the Custodian's first announcement, the fight protocol, the fight log over time. As bosses are met they are filled in. By the end of Year One the practitioner has met five bosses and twice tested at the trials; the codex has seven spreads with handwritten history.

**The Logbook.** A weekly ledger. One spread per bead. Date, weight, sleep, the day's modality, the dice's roll, the work performed, notes. The Custodian's morning header at the top of each day. The bead's load prescription at the top of the spread.

Neither document is required. The practice runs without them. They are how the practice becomes a thing the practitioner can hold in their hands in ten years.

## Constraints

- **Six days per week.** Sunday is Sanctum.
- **Condo apartment.** Carpeted floor. Downstairs neighbors. No jumping, no plyometrics, no dropped weights.
- **No equipment** in Year One. A floor, a wall, a doorway frame. Bodyweight progressions only.
- **Air Force PT test components** are the Year One benchmark — pushups, situps, plank, 1.5-mile run.
- **Thirty-year horizon.** The training is calibrated for sustainability, not peak performance.
