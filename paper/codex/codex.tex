% ============ INSIDE FRONT =================================================
{\sffamily\bfseries\small THE CODEX}\par
\vspace{4pt}\hrule height 0.4pt\vspace{6pt}
\footnotesize
The Codex is the permanent record. One spread per boss. RELAY's first contact
transcribed by hand. The fight protocol printed. The fight log filled in
ink over the operator's years.

Each boss can be re-fought any Sunday Sanctum for a personal-record attempt,
or surfaced as a \emph{haunt} by the dice on a low probability. Either way,
the row in the fight log gets added.
